# 🚗 Automotive Cybersecurity Case Study
### Ethical, Legal, Professional & Technical Analysis of the 2015 Jeep Cherokee Hack

> A cybersecurity case study exploring one of the most influential vehicle hacking incidents that changed the automotive industry's approach to cybersecurity.

---

## 📖 Overview

Modern vehicles are no longer isolated mechanical machines—they are connected cyber-physical systems.

This project analyzes the **2015 Jeep Cherokee remote hacking incident**, where researchers successfully compromised a moving vehicle through its internet-connected infotainment system.

Rather than focusing only on the technical exploit, this report investigates the incident from multiple cybersecurity perspectives including:

- Technical vulnerabilities
- Ethical responsibilities
- Professional conduct
- Legal implications
- Privacy concerns
- Social impact
- Future security recommendations

The project demonstrates how cybersecurity extends beyond technology into governance, public safety, and responsible disclosure.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand how modern connected vehicles can be compromised
- Analyze the Jeep Cherokee attack methodology
- Evaluate ethical responsibilities of security researchers
- Examine applicable cybersecurity laws and regulations
- Assess the impact on manufacturers and consumers
- Recommend security improvements for future connected vehicles

## 🔍 Key Takeaways

- Connected vehicles significantly increase the attack surface.
- Security should be integrated during vehicle design rather than added later.
- Responsible disclosure plays a critical role in protecting public safety.
- Ethical and legal considerations are equally important as technical expertise.
- Cybersecurity is now a fundamental requirement for modern transportation systems.
